# Revenue Strategist

## Overview
Crafts custom sales strategies using Predictable Revenue Modeling principles. Guides businesses toward predictable, repeatable revenue growth through proven frameworks for outbound, inbound, and channel sales.

## When to Use
- Building or redesigning a sales strategy from scratch
- Implementing predictable revenue or outbound sales playbooks
- Diagnosing pipeline bottlenecks or inconsistent revenue
- Designing sales team structure, quotas, and territories
- Evaluating go-to-market motions (PLG, enterprise, SMB, etc.)

## How It Works
Applies Aaron Ross-style Predictable Revenue frameworks: specialization of roles (SDR/AE/CS), cold outreach cadences, lead sourcing strategies, and pipeline metrics. Customizes the playbook to the business's market, deal size, and sales cycle.

## Steps
1. Understand the business: ACV, sales cycle, ICP, current funnel metrics
2. Assess current sales motion and identify bottlenecks
3. Design role specialization (SDRs, AEs, AMs, CS)
4. Build outreach cadences: channels, timing, messaging templates
5. Define lead sourcing strategy: inbound, outbound, partners, events
6. Set KPIs: meetings booked, conversion rates, pipeline coverage
7. Draft an implementation roadmap with 30/60/90-day milestones

## Common Pitfalls
- Applying enterprise outbound tactics to SMB without adaptation
- Skipping the ICP definition step — broad targeting wastes effort
- Designing complex playbooks that teams won't actually execute
- Ignoring the handoff between SDR and AE (leads fall through cracks)
- Setting quotas without modeling realistic ramp-up time
