# Newsletter Assistant

## Overview
Creates bold, high-impact newsletters with a distinct editorial voice. Handles everything from daily AI industry digests to weekly curated roundups — content curation, copywriting, formatting, and subject line optimization. Consolidates Unstuck Newsletter Assistant and AI Newsletter Assistant.

## When to Use
- Creating a daily AI/tech industry digest newsletter
- Curating and summarizing top articles, papers, or products of the week
- Writing a personal or brand newsletter with a specific editorial voice
- Generating newsletter content from a set of links, tweets, or notes
- Optimizing existing newsletter: subject lines, open rates, section structure
- Designing newsletter templates for consistent brand cadence

## How It Works
1. Define the newsletter's voice, audience, and cadence.
2. Curate or receive content to feature.
3. Structure the edition: headline, intro, curated items with commentary, CTA, footer.
4. Write in the specified voice — bold, analytical, witty, professional, etc.
5. Optimize subject line and preview text for open rates.

## Steps
1. **Voice & audience definition** — Clarify who the newsletter is for and what tone they respond to. Name the voice (e.g., "insider analyst," "cheerful curator," "provocative thought leader").
2. **Content curation** — If links are provided, review and prioritize. If not provided, suggest categories and topics based on the audience.
3. **Edition structure** — Build the skeleton: subject line, preview text, intro blurb, 3-5 curated items (headline + 2-3 sentence summary + "why it matters"), sponsor/ad slot if applicable, CTA, unsubscribe footer.
4. **Writing** — Draft each section in the target voice. Make it scannable: bold key phrases, use em dashes, keep summaries tight.
5. **Subject line workshop** — Generate 5+ subject line variants using different hooks: curiosity, urgency, listicle, question, bold statement.
6. **Polish** — Check for typos, link accuracy, consistent voice, and mobile readability (short paragraphs).

## Common Pitfalls
- **Voice drift**: Switching between casual and formal mid-edition confuses readers. Pick one voice and commit.
- **Over-curation**: Including 20+ links overwhelms. Curate ruthlessly — 3-5 high-quality items beats 15 mediocre ones.
- **Weak subject lines**: The subject line is 80% of open-rate leverage. Invest disproportionate effort here.
- **No "why it matters"**: Summaries that just describe the article add no value. Always include your angle.
- **Forgetting mobile**: 60%+ of newsletters are read on mobile. Keep paragraphs short, CTAs thumb-tappable.
