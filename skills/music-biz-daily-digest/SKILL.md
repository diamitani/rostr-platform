# Music Biz Daily Digest

## Overview
Creates a curated daily digest of the most important music industry business news, tailored for independent artists. Covers streaming platform changes, industry deals, legal/legislative updates, platform monetization shifts, emerging tools, and career-relevant developments — summarized for quick consumption.

## When to Use
- An artist wants to stay informed about industry changes that affect their career
- Creating a recurring news brief for an artist community or newsletter
- Researching current trends before making a business decision
- Starting the day with a quick industry scan

## How It Works
1. **Scan sources**: Review major music industry news sources for the day's top stories.
2. **Filter for relevance**: Prioritize news that impacts independent artists (not just major label deals).
3. **Summarize**: Write 1-paragraph summaries of 5-8 top stories.
4. **Format**: Clean digest format with headlines, summaries, and source links.
5. **Add "Why It Matters"**: A one-liner explaining the takeaway for indie artists.

## Steps
1. Check top music industry sources: Billboard, Music Business Worldwide, Hypebot, Digital Music News, Variety Music.
2. Identify 5-8 stories most relevant to independent artists.
3. Write each entry with: headline, 2-3 sentence summary, source link, and a "Why It Matters" takeaway.
4. Group by category if helpful (Streaming, Legal, Tech/Tools, Opportunities).
5. Deliver as a clean digest, suitable for reading or sharing.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Too much noise**: Don't include every press release. Filter ruthlessly for actionable relevance.
- **No context**: Always explain why a story matters to an indie artist, not just what happened.
- **Forgetting opportunities**: Include open calls, grant deadlines, and submission windows — these are high-value.
- **Stale news**: Daily means daily. If you can't produce a current digest, be upfront about the date of the information.
