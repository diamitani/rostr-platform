# Credit Report Guide

Walk the consumer through pulling their own credit reports correctly. Procedural guidance only — never ask for or handle their SSN, full report contents, or login credentials.

## AnnualCreditReport.com (official — use this first)

https://www.annualcreditreport.com is the only federally authorized source for free reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion.

1. Go to https://www.annualcreditreport.com (beware impostor sites — check the URL).
2. Click "Request your credit reports."
3. Enter name, date of birth, SSN, and current address (the consumer types this themselves — never collect it).
4. Select **all three**: Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion.
5. Complete each bureau's identity-verification quiz separately.
6. **Save each report as PDF immediately** (Print → Save as PDF) before closing the tab — the same view may not be available again.
7. Alternatives: phone 1-877-322-8228; mail: Annual Credit Report Request Service, P.O. Box 105281, Atlanta, GA 30348-5281.

## Bureau-direct reports (supplemental)

Each bureau also offers free reports on its own site — useful when AnnualCreditReport.com verification fails:
- Equifax: equifax.com/personal
- Experian: experian.com
- TransUnion: transunion.com

## Specialty / "fourth" bureaus (separate free annual reports)

- **Innovis** — fourth major bureau; request at innovis.com
- **LexisNexis** — data-broker reports used in background checks
- **ChexSystems** — bank-account reporting (matters for opening new accounts)

## What NOT to use for disputes

- **Credit Karma / WalletHub PDFs are summaries, not full bureau files** — parsing may miss tradelines. They show VantageScore, not the FICO scores most lenders use.
- Always dispute from the full bureau report (AnnualCreditReport.com PDF or bureau-direct), not a monitoring-app summary.

## After pulling: next steps

1. Save all three PDFs with dated filenames (`2026-09-16_Equifax.pdf`).
2. Hand the reports to the Report Analyzer / Credit Report Examiner flow for categorization.
3. Note the pull date — disputes reference "the report dated X."

## Guardrails

- **Never ask for, collect, or store** the consumer's SSN, full report text, or bureau login credentials.
- **Not legal advice.** This is procedural guidance for obtaining one's own reports.
- Remind the consumer to pull from a private device and secure the PDFs.
