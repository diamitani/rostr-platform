# Artist Resource Hub

## Overview
A comprehensive guide to music industry resources, tools, opportunities, and deals for independent artists. Covers distribution platforms, funding sources, educational materials, networking opportunities, software discounts, and industry programs — acting as a one-stop resource directory.

## When to Use
- An artist asks "What tools/resources are available for independent musicians?"
- An artist needs to find funding, grants, or sponsorship opportunities
- Searching for industry conferences, workshops, or networking events
- Comparing distribution platforms, PROs, or royalty collection services
- Finding artist discounts on software, gear, and services

## How It Works
1. **Understand the need**: What kind of resource is the artist looking for?
2. **Search and compile**: Gather relevant resources from known directories, programs, and platforms.
3. **Organize**: Present resources by category with clear descriptions, costs, and eligibility.
4. **Prioritize**: Highlight free and high-value options first.

## Steps
1. Ask: "What kind of resources are you looking for? (funding, tools, education, networking, distribution)"
2. Compile relevant resources by category:
   - **Distribution**: DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, Amuse, UnitedMasters (compare pricing, features).
   - **Funding & grants**: PRS Foundation, Help Musicians, MusiCares, local arts councils, Kickstarter.
   - **Education**: Berklee Online free courses, Coursera music business, YouTube channels (e.g., Adam Ivy, Damian Keyes).
   - **Tools & software**: DAW discounts (Ableton, Logic, FL Studio), Splice, LANDR, Canva for artists.
   - **PROs & collections**: ASCAP, BMI, SESAC (US); PRS, PPL (UK); SOCAN (Canada) — plus SoundExchange, Songtrust.
   - **Conferences & networking**: SXSW, A3C, Reeperbahn, Music Biz, local meetups.
   - **Playlist pitching**: Spotify for Artists, SubmitHub, Groover, PlaylistPush.
3. Present in a clean directory format with name, description, cost, and link.
4. Flag "pro-tip" resources that are particularly valuable or underutilized.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Outdated info**: Deals expire, platforms change pricing. Acknowledge that info should be verified.
- **US-centric bias**: Include international resources (UK, Canada, EU, Australia) when relevant.
- **Overwhelm**: Don't dump 100 links. Curate to the top 5-10 most relevant.
- **No context**: Explain why each resource is useful, not just what it is.
