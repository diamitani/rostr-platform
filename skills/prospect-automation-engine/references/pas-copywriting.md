# Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS) AI Copywriting Playbook

This reference provides the copywriting guidelines, system prompts, variables, and sector-specific templates used by the AI reasoning node (Node 05).

---

## 🎯 The 3-Sentence PAS Formula

Every cold email generated by the Prospect Automation Engine must adhere to the 3-Sentence PAS structure:

1. **Sentence 1 — Problem (Observation & Relevance)**:
   - Identify a verifiable fact about the prospect's company, department, or recent milestone.
   - *Example:* "Saw that CloudFlow is actively scaling your outbound sales team this quarter with new SDR openings."

2. **Sentence 2 — Agitate (Quantified Pain & Friction)**:
   - Articulate the specific time, revenue, or pipeline leakage experienced by leaders in their role.
   - *Example:* "Most VPs of Sales we speak with lose 28% of qualified pipeline because reps spend 15+ hours weekly copying CSVs between tools instead of talking to prospects."

3. **Sentence 3 — Solve (Low-Friction Solution & Soft CTA)**:
   - Introduce the company offer with pricing clarity and a low-commitment call to action.
   - *Example:* "Acme DataSync automates real-time pipeline enrichment and instant sequence routing for $49/user/mo. Open to a brief 5-minute peek before next week's team sync?"
