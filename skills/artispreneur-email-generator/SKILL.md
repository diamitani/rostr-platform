# Artispreneur Email Generator

## Overview

Generates professional, campaign-ready emails for music artists. Part of the Artispreneur ecosystem. Crafts emails for playlist pitching, blog outreach, venue booking, fan engagement, press, and label inquiries.

## When to Use

- Artist needs to write outreach emails to playlist curators, blogs, or press
- Crafting fan newsletter or announcement emails
- Writing booking inquiries for venues
- Building a music marketing email campaign
- Following up on previous outreach

## How It Works

Takes the artist's context (genre, release, goals, target) and generates tailored email copy with appropriate tone, structure, and call-to-action for music industry recipients.

## Steps

1. **Identify the campaign type** — Playlist pitch, blog feature, venue booking, press release, fan update, or label inquiry
2. **Gather artist context** — Genre, recent release, key stats, links, and artistic identity
3. **Define the recipient** — Who is this email going to and what do they care about
4. **Craft the email** — Subject line, greeting, body, social proof, call-to-action, signature
5. **Provide variants** — Offer A/B test versions with different angles
6. **Include follow-up** — Generate a polite follow-up email for non-responders

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Too generic** — Music industry recipients get flooded; emails need specific personalization
2. **Wrong tone for recipient** — A blog pitch vs. a label inquiry require very different approaches
3. **Missing links** — Always include streaming links, EPK, and social proof
4. **No clear ask** — Every email needs a specific, low-friction call-to-action
5. **Overly long** — Industry people skim; keep it tight
