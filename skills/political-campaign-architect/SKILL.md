# Political Campaign Architect

## Overview

A comprehensive guide for planning and executing political campaigns — from strategy development to digital mobilization and election automation. Consolidates Political Campaign Architect (campaign structure & planning), Political Campaign Guide (tactical playbook), and Candidate Insight GPT (voter data & opposition research).

## When to Use

- Planning a political campaign from scratch (local, state, or federal)
- Developing a digital strategy for voter outreach and mobilization
- Building campaign infrastructure: volunteer networks, fundraising, communications
- Analyzing voter demographics and creating targeted messaging
- Conducting opposition research and candidate positioning
- Automating campaign operations (email, SMS, social media scheduling)
- Creating a campaign timeline with key milestones and GOTV plans

## How It Works

This skill provides a structured framework for political campaign architecture. Start with campaign fundamentals (mission, target audience, budget), then move into digital strategy, field operations, and GOTV planning. The skill guides you through each phase with actionable templates and checklists.

## Steps

### 1. Campaign Foundation

- Define the campaign's mission, vision, and core message
- Identify target voter demographics and key constituencies
- Set SMART goals: volunteer targets, fundraising milestones, voter contact numbers
- Establish campaign budget with allocations for staff, ads, field, and digital

### 2. Digital Strategy & Infrastructure

- Set up campaign website with donation processing and email capture
- Build social media presence across platforms (platform selection based on demographics)
- Create content calendar with themes, cadence, and platform-specific formats
- Configure email marketing: welcome series, donation asks, volunteer recruitment
- Plan digital ad strategy: platform selection, targeting, creative testing, budget pacing

### 3. Field Operations & Voter Contact

- Recruit and train volunteer coordinators
- Build voter contact universes (likely supporters, persuadable, opposition)
- Plan canvassing routes and phone banking shifts
- Implement voter ID and data tracking systems
- Design GOTV (Get Out The Vote) strategy: early voting, mail-in ballots, Election Day

### 4. Fundraising & Compliance

- Develop fundraising plan: small-dollar digital, events, major donors, PACs
- Set up compliance: FEC/state filing deadlines, contribution limits, reporting
- Create donor communication cadence and stewardship plan

### 5. Opposition Research & Positioning

- Research opponent's voting record, public statements, and vulnerabilities
- Prepare rapid response protocols for attacks
- Develop candidate positioning and debate preparation materials

### 6. Automation & Technology

- CRM setup for voter and donor management (NGP VAN, NationBuilder, etc.)
- Automate email sequences, SMS reminders, and social posting
- Set up analytics dashboards for real-time campaign performance tracking

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Starting digital too late** — Build your email list and social presence 6-12 months before Election Day.
2. **Neglecting compliance** — Missed FEC deadlines can result in fines and bad press. Assign a compliance officer early.
3. **Over-relying on one channel** — Diversify: digital ads, direct mail, door knocking, and earned media all matter.
4. **No rapid response plan** — Have pre-approved messaging and a chain of command for crises.
5. **Burning budget early** — Front-load critical infrastructure, but reserve 40-50% for the final 4 weeks.
