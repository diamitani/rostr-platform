# Social Media Marketing

The Monetization Wizard's distribution craft, used by Megaphone.
**Order: channel → cadence → hooks → loops → paid decision.**

## Hard rules

1. **Draft and guide only.** Never send, post, publish, pay, buy, or
   sign the user up for anything. You write the posts, hooks, and
   calendar; the user approves and publishes.
2. **Budget reality.** Every tactic carries a cost tag and a free
   alternative. At $0, the plan is organic and repurposing only.
3. **Vanity metrics are banned.** Track the metric tied to revenue.
4. **Paid spend gets a daily cap, a kill rule, and a test budget**
   before a single dollar scales.
5. **Never promise virality.** Promise consistency, hooks, and feedback
   loops.

## 1. Channel selection by product type + budget

Pick **one primary channel**. Name the runner-up and park it.

| Product type | Primary | Runner-up | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS / services | LinkedIn | X/Twitter | buyers live there; DMs convert |
| Creator / course / community | YouTube or X | Instagram | long-form trust → high-ticket |
| E-commerce / DTC | TikTok or Instagram | Pinterest | visual discovery → purchase |
| Local business | Instagram + Google Business | TikTok | geo + reviews |
| Dev tools / API | X/Twitter, GitHub | YouTube | builders in public |
| Newsletter / media | X/Twitter | LinkedIn | writers' network effects |
| Marketplace | TikTok/IG (demand) + niche forums (supply) | YouTube | two audiences, two motions |

**Budget overlay:**
- **$0:** organic only. Pick the channel where you can post daily
  without production cost (usually X or LinkedIn text).
- **<$500:** organic + one boost test ($5–10/day on the single
  best-performing organic post).
- **$500–2k:** organic + small always-on paid test ($20–50/day, one
  audience, one creative).
- **$2k–10k:** organic + paid with creative testing (3–5 creatives,
  kill losers weekly).
- **$10k+:** full-funnel paid + organic engine; hire or agency territory
  (Pennywise must see the math first).

**Channel kill rule:** 30 days of the prescribed cadence with zero
revenue-adjacent signal (profile visits → link clicks → conversations)
= wrong channel or wrong message. Change one, not both.

## 2. 30-day content calendar template

Cadence rule: the calendar must be keepable. 3x/week kept beats 7x/week
abandoned.

```markdown
## 30-Day Calendar — [Channel] — [Month]
Primary metric: [link clicks / DMs / trials — revenue-adjacent]
Cadence: [3x/week: Mon/Wed/Fri]

Week 1 — Authority (teach)
- Mon: [hook] → [point] → [CTA: follow/save]
- Wed: [hook] → ...
- Fri: ...

Week 2 — Proof (show)
- Mon: result / case study / teardown ...
Week 3 — Personality (connect)
- Mon: story / opinion / behind-the-scenes ...
Week 4 — Conversion (ask)
- Mon: offer post / demo / testimonial ...

Repurposing: each week's best performer → [ladder below].
```

**Content pillars (rotate weekly):** Teach (how-to, frameworks),
Prove (results, teardowns, numbers), Connect (stories, opinions,
behind-the-scenes), Ask (offers, CTAs — max 20% of posts).

## 3. Hook library (30+)

A hook's job: stop the scroll in the first line. Steal the structure,
write your own specifics.

**Curiosity:**
1. "I [did X] for 30 days. Here's what actually happened:"
2. "Nobody talks about [uncomfortable truth in your niche]."
3. "The [tool/tactic] everyone recommends is wrong. Here's why:"
4. "I asked 50 [buyers] how they [job]. The answers surprised me."

**Numbers:**
5. "How I went from [bad] to [good] in [timeframe]:"
6. "3 [mistakes] costing [buyers] $[X]/month:"
7. "I spent $[X] testing [thing]. Steal my results:"
8. "[X]% of [buyers] do [thing] wrong. Are you one?"

**Contrarian:**
9. "Stop [popular advice]. Do this instead:"
10. "Unpopular opinion: [take]."
11. "[Guru] is wrong about [topic]."
12. "The best [outcome] strategy is the one nobody posts about."

**Story:**
13. "In [year], I [failure]. Last month, I [win]. The difference:"
14. "A client told me [quote]. They were right."
15. "I almost quit [thing] in [month]. Glad I didn't:"

**How-to:**
16. "The exact [process] I use to [outcome] (steal it):"
17. "[Outcome] in [time]: a 5-step checklist:"
18. "Beginner's guide to [topic] — everything I wish I knew:"
19. "Copy-paste [template] for [outcome]:"

**Question:**
20. "What's the ONE [tool/habit] you'd never give up?"
21. "Hot take: [question]?"
22. "If you could only [constraint], what would you do?"

**Proof:**
23. "Screenshot: [result]. Here's the full breakdown:"
24. "Client results after [timeframe] (with numbers):"
25. "We tested [A] vs [B]. [B] won by [X]%."

**Direct:**
26. "If you're a [buyer] struggling with [pain], read this."
27. "POV: you're [painful situation]. Here's the fix:"
28. "[Buyer]: this one's for you."
29. "Tag someone who needs to hear [truth]."
30. "Save this for the next time you [situation]."

**Offer (use sparingly):**
31. "I'm opening [X] spots for [offer]. Details:"
32. "Last chance: [offer] closes [date]."

**Hook rules:** specific beats clever; numbers beat adjectives; the
first 8 words decide everything. Never use a hook you can't deliver on
in the post — curiosity gaps are fine, clickbait is not.

## 4. Growth loops (pick one, run it 90 days)

- **Content → lead magnet → email → offer.** Every post points to one
  free asset; the asset sells the offer. The compounding loop.
- **Reply → profile → DM → call.** Be the most useful replier in your
  niche's comments for 30 days; the profile does the selling.
- **Customer → testimonial → content → customer.** Systematize the ask:
  every win becomes next month's proof post.
- **Partner → audience swap → partner.** Monthly swaps with adjacent
  (non-competing) audiences: guest posts, joint lives, bundle promos.
- **SEO → free tool → signup.** One genuinely useful free tool or
  calculator; it ranks, it converts, it never sleeps. (Engineering cost
  — Pennywise tags it.)
- **Community → UGC → reach → community.** Seed discussions; members'
  posts recruit members. Works only after 50+ real members.

## 5. Paid-vs-organic decision tree

```
Can you afford to lose the test budget with zero return?
├─ No → ORGANIC ONLY. (No exceptions. Not "just $50.")
└─ Yes ↓
    Is LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1 proven on organic/small tests?
    ├─ No → ORGANIC ONLY until the math proves. Paid amplifies;
    │         it does not fix a broken offer.
    └─ Yes ↓
        Start: $X/day (test budget = 3 × target CAC × days to learn)
        One audience. One creative. Kill rule set in writing.
        Scale 30–50%/week only while CAC holds within 20% of target.
```

**Paid creative rules:** UGC-style beats polished 9 times in 10;
hook in the first 2 seconds; one CTA; landing page must match the ad's
promise exactly (message match = conversion).

## 6. Content repurposing ladder

One idea → five assets. Never create from scratch twice.

1. **Long-form** (newsletter, YouTube, blog) — the source of truth.
2. **Thread / carousel** — the 5–7 key points, native to the channel.
3. **Short video** (60–90s) — the single best point, face to camera.
4. **Quote graphics / screenshots** — the numbers and one-liners.
5. **Replies and comments** — the idea deployed as answers for a week.

**Weekly repurposing ritual (1 hour, FREE):** take the week's best
performer → run it down the ladder → schedule. One great idea properly
repurposed outperforms five mediocre originals.

## 7. Metrics that matter (by stage)

| Stage | Track | Ignore |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1k followers | DMs started, link clicks, replies | follower count |
| 1k–10k | email signups, trial starts | likes |
| 10k+ | revenue attributed, CAC by channel | impressions |

**Weekly review (20 min, FREE):** top 3 posts by the revenue-adjacent
metric → what do they have in common → do more of that; bottom 3 →
what to stop. One experiment for next week. Log it.
