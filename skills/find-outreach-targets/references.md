# Outreach playbook — references for find-outreach-targets

## The one rule

Bookers and curators say yes to artists who make their job easy: the
right genre, the right room size, links that work, and a message that
proves you know who they are. Everything below serves that.

## Playlist curators

- Keep it under 120 words. Curators skim.
- Subject: `Submission: <Artist> — <Track> (<Genre>)`
- Body: 1 line on why this playlist (name a recent add or the
  playlist's stated vibe), 1 line on the track (mood, comparable
  artist — honest comps only), link, 1-line bio.
- Never: "this will blow up", "guaranteed streams", follow-ups before
  7 days, submitting the same track twice.
- Spotify editorial is pitched via Spotify for Artists, not email —
  say so when the target is editorial.

## Venue bookers / promoters

- They care about: draw (be honest — "50–80 in Chicago" beats
  silence), dates (give 3 options), assets (EPK link, live video if
  you have it), and whether you're easy to work with.
- Subject: `Booking: <Artist> — <City> <Month> (<Draw> draw)`
- Body: dates, draw, 1-line pitch, links, tech needs in one line
  ("4-piece, backline needed" or "self-contained duo").
- Never pitch a 500-cap room with no draw history. The skill's tier
  scoring exists to prevent exactly this.

## Radio / press

- Angle first: why this story, why now, why them. "New single out
  Friday" is not an angle. "Chicago drill artist's first Spanish-language
  single, premiering during Hispanic Heritage Month" is.
- Give them everything in one email: stream link, download link,
  100-word bio, hi-res photo link, release date, contact.

## Follow-ups

- Exactly one, 7 days later, shorter than the first: "Floating this
  back up — <track> is out <date>. Still a fit?"
- No response after that = no. Never a third email.

## Compliance

- Every message has a real reply-to and a one-line opt-out
  ("Reply 'remove' and I won't email again").
- One-to-one tailored outreach to publicly listed professional
  contacts. This is not a bulk-email tool and must never be used
  as one — the skill refuses batch sends over 20/day.
