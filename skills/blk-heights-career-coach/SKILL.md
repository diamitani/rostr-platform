# BLK Heights Career Coach

## Overview

Empowering career coaching and content guidance specifically designed for tech-driven communities. Powered by blkheights.com, this skill helps professionals navigate career transitions, build personal brands, create compelling content, and develop leadership presence in the technology industry. Focuses on culturally competent, community-centered career development.

## When to Use

- Navigating career transitions within the tech industry
- Building a personal brand and professional online presence
- Creating content (articles, posts, talks) that establishes thought leadership
- Preparing for salary negotiations and promotion conversations
- Developing a career roadmap: skills to build, roles to target, network to cultivate
- Finding mentors and sponsors in tech
- Overcoming impostor syndrome and building professional confidence
- Planning side projects or entrepreneurial ventures in tech

## Steps

### 1. Career Assessment

- Evaluate current role, skills, and satisfaction level
- Identify career goals: promotion, pivot, entrepreneurship, thought leadership
- Map skills gaps and create a learning plan
- Define your unique value proposition as a tech professional

### 2. Personal Brand Building

- Audit your current online presence (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, social)
- Define your brand pillars: what you want to be known for
- Create a content strategy: posting cadence, topics, formats
- Build a portfolio that showcases projects with measurable impact

### 3. Content Creation Guidance

- Identify content topics at the intersection of your expertise and community needs
- Structure thought leadership pieces: problem → insight → actionable takeaway
- Choose formats: LinkedIn posts, Twitter/X threads, blog posts, talks, workshops
- Distribution strategy: where your audience is and how to reach them

### 4. Networking & Community

- Build authentic relationships, not transactional connections
- Identify communities, conferences, and events aligned with your goals
- Give before you ask: share knowledge, make introductions, provide value
- Find mentors and sponsors: who to approach, how to frame the ask

### 5. Career Advancement Tactics

- Document your impact: track metrics, save praise, build a brag document
- Time promotion conversations around review cycles and business impact
- Negotiate compensation using market data and your documented contributions
- Know when to stay, when to level up internally, and when to leave

### 6. Entrepreneurship & Side Projects

- Validate ideas before building: talk to potential users first
- Start small: build an MVP, get feedback, iterate
- Leverage your network for early customers, advisors, and co-founders
- Balance a side project with a full-time role: time management and energy protection

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Waiting to be noticed** — Document your wins and advocate for yourself. Nobody else will track your impact.
2. **Networking only when you need something** — Build relationships continuously, not just during job searches.
3. **Content without consistency** — One viral post won't build a brand. Commit to a sustainable cadence.
4. **Undervaluing community** — Your network is your greatest career asset. Invest in it generously.
5. **Comparing your Chapter 1 to someone's Chapter 10** — Focus on your own trajectory and growth.
